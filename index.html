How to Play

  • Tap anywhere to fire the solar cannon at that spot. Every hit scores points — there's no wrong target.
  • 🪟 Broken panels, 👾 energy monsters, 💸 electricity bills, ☁️ clouds and ⚡ power thieves drift across the screen — blast them all before they get away. Let one drift off unhit and it costs you 15 points and breaks your streak, so keep firing.
  • ☀️ Solar charges your streak, 🔋 batteries give you rapid-fire for a few seconds, and 💰 energy coins are pure bonus points.
  • 60 seconds, three stages: easy and slow to start, faster at 40 seconds left, and everything goes crazy in the last 20 — that's 🚨 MEGA POWER MODE.
  • When the clock hits zero, the ☀️ Solar Blast finale auto-detonates everything left on screen for one last escalating burst of points.
  • Chain hits without missing for a streak bonus — the longer the streak, the more each hit is worth, with a big on-screen callout every 5 in a row.
  • Watch for a 🌟 golden glow — any target or collectible can occasionally spawn golden, worth triple points.
